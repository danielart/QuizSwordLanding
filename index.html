Info

QuizSword combines the fun of retro gaming with the power of learning. Create flashcards, study units, and conquer challenges in a medieval-themed world.

Whether you're studying for exams, learning new skills, or expanding your knowledge, QuizSword transforms the process into an epic adventure.

Features

Customizable Flashcards

Create your own flashcards with text, images, and custom formatting to enhance your learning experience.

Achievements & Rankings

Earn badges, level up, and climb the global leaderboards as you master your study materials.

Social Features

Share decks, challenge friends, and join guilds to make learning a collaborative adventure.

Pixel Art Medieval Theme

Immerse yourself in a charming retro-style world with knights, castles, and magical elements.
